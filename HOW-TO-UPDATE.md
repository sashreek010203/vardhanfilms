# How to update the website (no coding needed)

You update the site by filling in simple forms, like editing a Google Form.
There is no code to touch. When you press **Save**, the live website updates
by itself within about a minute.

---

## Opening the editor

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** (use the GitHub account the website is stored in).
3. Pick the website from the list.

On the left you will see three sections:

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| 🎬 **Films & Projects** | Every film, series and ad, with video links |
| 🏆 **Laurels & Awards** | The festival laurels |
| 👤 **Profile, About & Contact** | Name, photo, bio, showreel, phone, email, CV, social links |

---

## Add a new film

1. Open **🎬 Films & Projects**.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the list and click **Add an entry**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**: the film's name
   - **Your role(s)**: pick Director, Editor, etc. (you can pick more than one)
   - **Type of project**: Feature Film, Short Film, Documentary…
   - **Video link**: paste the YouTube or Vimeo link, *or leave it empty*
   - **Year** and **Short note** are optional
4. Click **Save**.

What happens automatically:
- **With a video link**, the film appears in *Selected Work* as a playable frame,
  and the YouTube picture is used as its thumbnail.
- **Without a video link**, it still appears in the *Filmography* credits list.
- Tick **Show bigger at the top** to feature it as a large frame.
- Set **Status** to *Work in Progress* to show a small red "In post" tag.

**To change the order:** drag entries up or down in the list. The site follows that order.
**To remove a film:** open its menu (⋯) and choose **Remove**, then **Save**.

---

## Add a new award or festival selection

1. Open **🏆 Laurels & Awards** and click **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Result**: e.g. *Best Film*, *Winner*, *2nd Place · Best Editing*, *Official Selection*
   - **Festival name** and **Year**
   - **For which film**: optional
3. **Save**.

Wins are drawn in **gold**. Anything with "Selection" or "Nominated" in the
result is drawn in **silver**. This happens automatically.

---

## Change the photo, showreel, CV, phone or social links

Open **👤 Profile, About & Contact**:

- **Main photo**: click the photo field and upload a new picture.
  Black-and-white, landscape (wide) photos with the face on the right look best.
- **Showreel link**: paste the Vimeo or YouTube link. The *Play Showreel*
  button only shows when this is filled in.
- **CV (PDF)**: upload a new PDF to replace the *Download CV* link.
- **Instagram / Facebook / LinkedIn**: paste the full link (starting with
  `https://`). Empty ones are simply hidden.
- **About me**: leave an empty line between paragraphs.

Then **Save**.

---

## Good to know

- **You can't break the design.** The forms only change words, links and pictures.
- **Mistakes are easy to undo.** Every save is kept in history, so an older version can always be restored.
- Changes usually appear on the live site **within 1–2 minutes**. If you don't
  see them, refresh the page (on a phone, pull down to refresh).
- **Video won't play inside the site?** Some YouTube videos are age-restricted or
  have embedding turned off by the uploader. Visitors can still click
  **Open on YouTube ↗** under the player. To make it play on the site, change the
  video's settings on YouTube (*Allow embedding*, no age restriction).
- **Thumbnail looks wrong?** Upload your own picture in **Custom thumbnail**.
